Toca Ficha Dr. — UI/UX Ideas
6 concrete suggestions to evaluate before shipping
post-v3.2.0 · 2026-05-07
The Prontuário tab now stacks 9 sections vertically, several added in v3.1.6–v3.2.0 (Modelos G-Hosp, Carta de orientação panel, SOAP recovery button, Atestado finalize bar). At 230 px wide that's a lot of scroll. These six ideas trade implementation effort for layout simplicity and signal clarity. Mocks use the same brand palette and components as the actual side panel.
1
Collapse "Modelos G-Hosp" + "Receita — Modelos" into one tabbed section
~2 h High Recommend
Two prescription-template surfaces stacked vertically (G-Hosp's server-side templates + the doctor's own Simples-flow templates) is the biggest visual offender. They share a mental model: "pick a template, run it." A 2-tab toggle inside one section halves the vertical space and clarifies the pick-by-source decision.

2
Streaming token counter → animated progress bar
~1 h Medium
"Gerando SOAP… 47 palavras" is honest but text-heavy. A thin pulsing progress bar under the record button reads as "I'm doing something" in a single glance. The word count can stay on the right edge of the bar as a fine print.

3
SOAP recovery button → inline 📋 icon next to status text
~30 min Low-Med
Currently the "📋 Copiar SOAP novamente" recovery button takes a full row when shown. An inline icon button next to the status line keeps the recovery affordance without consuming vertical space. Same user-gesture flow; less weight.

4
Letter mode panel → slide-down drawer below Atestado action button
~2 h High Recommend
Today the Carta de orientação panel lives in its own section between Modelos and Ações. That visually decouples it from its trigger (the standard "Atestado" button it's parallel to). Folding it into a drawer that opens directly under the Atestado/✉ row keeps the spatial relationship obvious.

5
Live audio waveform during recording
~3 h High Recommend
`attachVisualizer()` exists in content/audio-capture.js from the HUD era but was never re-wired in the v3.1+ side panel. Without a visible mic-active signal, doctors don't trust that recording is actually happening. Plan: broadcast analyser bins from the content script to the side panel at ~30 Hz, render in a small canvas next to the record button.

6
Config tab — "Automatismos" toggles group
~1 h Low-Med
v3.1.6 (encaminh prefill) and v3.1.7 (companion auto-fill) added storage flags autoPrefillEncaminh and autoFillCompanion, both opt-out via DevTools only. Doctors who want a different default need a UI. One labeled group with three checkboxes (autoCid, autoFillCompanion, autoPrefillEncaminh) closes the gap.
