<objective>
Close two HUD-state issues from the review:

1. **P1-5** — `finalizeSimplesPrescription` and the per-template prescription button handlers are async but have no concurrency guard. Double-click on a slow Cloudflare connection saves and prints the prescription twice. Add a mutex (state flag + button `.disabled = true`) on both entry points.

2. **P1-7** — The `chrome.storage.onChanged` listener registered in `setupEvents` is never removed. On SPA re-navigations that re-run the content script IIFE, old listeners stack; the newest HUD shows fresh templates but older detached HUDs also fire the update handler.

Purpose: eliminate duplicate prescription submissions and stop listener accumulation across SPA nav cycles.
Output: updated `content/hud.js` with mutexes on finalize/template buttons and tracked/removed storage listener.
</objective>
